## FAQ page (`page.faq.json`)

### faq-full
- **heading:** Every question worth asking.
- **subheading:** Everything you need to know before your first thread.

- _topic_header_
  - **topic_title:** Getting started
- _faq_item_
  - **question:** What is Threaditate?
  - **answer:** Threaditate is a crafting kit for turning a photo you love into string art by hand. You upload the photo to the app to convert it into a thread pattern, and you follow the spoken instructions line by line on a 20-inch loom until your picture appears over six hours. No experience needed. Everything - the loom, the thread, and the app - ships in one box.
- _faq_item_
  - **question:** Can I use any photo?
  - **answer:** Yes! Although, some photos work better than others- try many! Crisp portraits and close-ups with clear contrast tend to come out best - the app reads light and shadow rather than fine detail. A favorite person, a pet, a pastime that means something to you. Upload it, and the app does the rest.
- _faq_item_
  - **question:** Do I need any experience?
  - **answer:** None. The app walks you through every step, one thread at a time, like an audio paint by number. There's nothing to figure out on your own. The first step is the same for everyone and the result always wows.

- _topic_header_
  - **topic_title:** The kit
- _faq_item_
  - **question:** What's in the kit?
  - **answer:** Your kit comes with a 20-inch loom with 240 pins, a number guide, thread, spool holder, phone holder, detail wand, foam resistance plug, mounts, thread, and access to the Threaditate app. Open the box and you have everything you need to start in five minutes.
- _faq_item_
  - **question:** Do I need the app?
  - **answer:** Yes, the app is what makes Threaditate tick. Without it, you have string art. With it, you have a thread art session: image editing and real-time instructions, one step at a time, on iPhone and Android. Access is included with every kit.
- _faq_item_
  - **question:** Do I need any other tools or supplies?
  - **answer:** Nothing extra. The loom, the pins, the thread, and the app are all you need. Find a comfortable spot with good light, and you're ready to begin. Choice of tea, aromas, and background music are up to you though.

- _topic_header_
  - **topic_title:** The process
- _faq_item_
  - **question:** How long does it take?
  - **answer:** Most pieces take six to eight hours, depending on the linecount- each 500 lines takes about an hour, and we recommend 3000-4000 lines. Pause whenever, you don't have to finish in one sitting. The app saves your place after every step, so you can take it at your own pace.
- _faq_item_
  - **question:** What if I lose my place?
  - **answer:** The app remembers exactly where you stopped, down to the last thread you confirmed. Step away for an hour or a week, then pick up right where you left off.
- _faq_item_
  - **question:** What if I make a mistake?
  - **answer:** Carry on from your last confirmed step. Mistakes disappear into the finished piece — the overall image is what your eye sees, not the individual threads. Carry on with grace.
- _faq_item_
  - **question:** What if the thread breaks?
  - **answer:** It rarely happens, but if it does, that means you're way too tight. If it breaks or your kitten claws it, just knot the two ends together in a dark area and keep going. The knot disappears against the shadow and won't show in your finished piece.
- _faq_item_
  - **question:** Will the loom warp?
  - **answer:** Only if the thread is under tension. Relax and drape loosely, as tightness adds up and may cause some bend, which looks cool too. The tension used is the only factor, thread mindfully for a flat finale or weave wildly for a slight warp. Both have different shadow effects.
- _faq_item_
  - **question:** Can I pause and come back to it?
  - **answer:** Anytime. The app holds your progress automatically, so a Threaditate fits around your life. Some people finish in an afternoon. Others thread a little each evening over a week or few.

- _topic_header_
  - **topic_title:** After you finish
- _faq_item_
  - **question:** How do I display my finished piece?
  - **answer:** Your finished piece is made to be seen. The 20-inch loom sits naturally on a wall, a shelf, or a window sill. Find a spot with good light and let the work speak for itself.
- _faq_item_
  - **question:** Can I make a new piece with the same loom?
  - **answer:** Yes. When you're ready for a new picture, remove the thread and start fresh with a new pattern on the same loom. There is enough thread for multiple pieces per kit. Each loom holds one piece at a time, so to display several at once, simply add more kits.
